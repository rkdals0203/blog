## 💡 Key Points

- IT projects must be viewed within a systems approach that considers business, organizational, and technological aspects
- Understanding organizational structures and culture is crucial for project success
- Stakeholder management and top management commitment are critical success factors
- Project and product life cycles provide frameworks for managing project phases
- Recent trends affecting IT project management include globalization, outsourcing, virtual teams, and agile methodologies
- Agile approaches like Scrum focus on collaboration, working software, and adaptability

## Detailed Content 📚

### 1️⃣ Systems View of Project Management

- **Systems Approach**: a holistic(전체론적) approach to management
    - Systems: **sets of interacting components** working in an environment to fulfill a purpose
    - Three components: systems philosophy, systems analysis, systems management
    - 3-Step practical approach: 
	    1. define scope 범위 정의
	    2. divide into components 요소 분해
	    3. identify issues 이슈 식별
- **Three-Sphere Model**:
    - Business (financial considerations)
    - Organization (people aspects)
    - Technology (technical elements)
    Most projects fail due to organizational issues rather than technical problems

### 2️⃣ Understanding Organizations

- **Four Frames of Organizations**:
    1. Structural frame: How the organization is structured, roles and responsibilities
    2. Human resources frame: Producing harmony between organization and worker needs
    3. Political frame: Competition for power, resources, and leadership
    4. Symbolic frame: Hidden meanings, culture, and symbols
    ![[Pasted image 20250410152743.png]]
- **Organizational Structures**:
    - Functional: Functional managers report to CEO
    - Project: Program managers report to CEO
    - Matrix: Middle ground where personnel report to multiple bosses
        - Can be weak, balanced, or strong matrix
        - Potential inefficiency when team members are assigned to multiple projects
    ![[Pasted image 20250410152834.png]]
- **Organizational Culture**: Shared assumptions, values, and behaviors
    - Ten characteristics (Robbins & Judge):
        - Member identity: Organization vs. profession(저는 삼성전자의 직원입니다 vs 저는 소프트웨어 개발자입니다)
        - Group emphasis: Teams vs. individuals (업무 활동의 구성 방식)
        - People focus: Effect on people vs. other goals(인간 중심적인가? 다른 목표 지향인가?)
        - Unit integration: Coordination vs. individual performance (서로 다른 단위 부서가 얼마나 조화롭게 협업하는가)
        - Control: Direct vs. indirect supervision
        - Risk tolerance: Risk-seeking vs. risk-averse
        - Reward criteria: Performance-based vs. seniority-based
        - Conflict tolerance: Open vs. closed to criticism
        - Means-ends orientation: Outcomes vs. processes
        - Open-systems focus: Response to external environment

### 3️⃣ Stakeholder Management

- Stakeholders can be internal or external, directly or indirectly involved
- Use four frames of organization(Structural, Human Resources, Political, Symbolic) can help meet stakeholder needs and expectations
- Top management commitment is crucial for project success
- Chief Information Officer (CIO) at high level helps IT projects succeed
- Opening case example: Tom's tablet initiative failed due to poor stakeholder management

### 4️⃣ Project and Product Life Cycles

- **Project Life Cycle**: Collection of project phases defining work, deliverables, people, and controls
    - Early phases: Low resource needs, high uncertainty, maximum stakeholder influence
    - Middle phases: Improved certainty, increased resources
    - Final phase: Ensuring requirements are met, getting client approval
- **Product Life Cycle Models**:
    - Waterfall model: Linear stages of development and support
    - Spiral model: Iterative approach
    - Prototyping model: Developing prototypes to clarify requirements
	![[Pasted image 20250410154844.png]]
- **Management Reviews**:
    - Phase exits, gate reviews, or kill points after each phase

### 5️⃣ Nature of IT Projects

- Diverse in size, complexity, products, application area, and resources
- Team members often have diverse backgrounds and skill sets
- Use diverse technologies that change rapidly
- May involve contract workers ("army of free agents")

### 6️⃣ Recent Trends in Project Management

- **Globalization**:
    - Issues: Communications, trust, work practices, tools
    - Suggestions: Project discipline, thinking globally but acting locally, collaboration over standardization
- **Outsourcing**:
    - Organizations use outsourcing to reduce costs and remain competitive
    - Offshoring: Outsourcing from another country
- **Virtual Teams**:
    - Advantages: Lower costs, more expertise, improved work/life balance
    - Disadvantages: Isolation, communication problems, technology dependence
- **Agile Project Management**:
    - Manifesto values:
        - Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
        - Working software over comprehensive documentation
        - Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
        - Responding to change over following a plan
    - **Scrum Framework**:
        - Product owner creates prioritized product backlog
        - Team selects sprint backlog items for each sprint (2-4 weeks)
        - Daily Scrum meetings to assess progress
        - ScrumMaster keeps team focused
        - Sprint ends with review and retrospective
        - Sprint produces potentially shippable product
        - Burndown chart tracks progress
