
## 💡 Key Points

- IT project management is a growing field with increasing job opportunities worldwide
- The course covers all 10 knowledge areas and 5 process groups of project management
- Project: temporary endeavors with defined objectives, unlike ongoing operations
- Both waterfall and agile methodologies will be covered in the course

## Detailed Content 📚

### 1️⃣ Project Fundamentals

- **Definition**: "A temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result"
- **Difference from Operations**: Projects end when objectives are reached, operations sustain the business
- **Examples of IT Projects**:
    - Creating a smartphone application
    - Developing a driverless car
    - Consolidating systems after bank acquisitions
    - Updating a university library book-loan system

### 2️⃣ Project Management Framework

- **Process Groups**:
    - Initiating
    - Planning
    - Executing
    - Monitoring and controlling
    - Closing
- **Knowledge Areas** (10):
    1. Project Integration Management
    2. Project Scope Management
    3. Project Schedule Management
    4. Project Cost Management
    5. Project Quality Management
    6. Project Resource Management
    7. Project Communication Management
    8. Project Risk Management
    9. Project Procurement Management
    10. Project Stakeholder Management

### 3️⃣ Project Management Methodologies

- **Waterfall**: Sequential approach (one by one)
- **Agile**: Quick implementation with repetitive cycles
